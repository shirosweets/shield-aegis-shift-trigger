# Security

Thank you for helping keep Shield Aegis Shift Trigger and its users safe. I take security
reports seriously and appreciate responsible disclosures.

## Report a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately through GitHub Security
Advisories using
["Report a vulnerability"](https://github.com/shirosweets/shield-aegis-shift-trigger/security/advisories/new).
Please do not report security issues in public issues or discussions.

## What to include

To help me understand and investigate the issue, please include, when possible:

- The affected version, release, or commit.
- A description of the potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce the issue safely, or a proof of concept.
- Relevant environment details and any logs that help explain what happened.

Please do not include real passwords, access tokens, private keys, or other
sensitive data in your report. Avoid actions that could affect other people's
systems or data.

## Testing boundaries

Please keep research safe and limited to systems and data you own or are
authorized to test. Do not:

- Perform denial-of-service or other disruptive testing.
- Send spam or conduct phishing or social engineering.
- Attempt to access, alter, or destroy data or systems without authorization.
- Target anyone's physical property or facilities.

If you are unsure whether a test is safe or authorized, stop and ask before
proceeding.

## What happens next

I will respond with next steps and may ask for more information. I will keep
you informed as I investigate and work toward a fix and, where appropriate, a
public announcement.

## Examples and test fixtures

Some examples and files under `tests/fixtures/` are intentionally vulnerable
and exist only to test the scanners. They are illustrative, may not be
maintained, and must not be used in production.

## Issues in third-party software

If you find a security issue in third-party software, please report it directly
to the person or team that maintains that software.

For more information about private vulnerability reporting, see GitHub's
[documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/how-tos/report-and-fix-vulnerabilities/report-privately).
